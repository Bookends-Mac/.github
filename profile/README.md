## What is Bookends for Mac?

Bookends for Mac is a **reference management software** tailored for academics, researchers, and students. It helps you collect, organize, and cite references with ease, supporting a wide range of citation styles including APA, MLA, Chicago, and more.

The software integrates seamlessly with word processors like Microsoft Word and Pages, allowing you to insert citations and generate bibliographies without leaving your document. Its powerful search functionality lets you quickly find references across your library, and its built-in PDF viewer supports annotations and highlights.

Bookends for Mac is optimized for **macOS Ventura, Sonoma, and later versions**, ensuring smooth performance on both Intel and Apple Silicon Macs. With features like auto-completion, duplicate detection, and cloud sync, it streamlines the research process from start to finish.

Whether you're writing a thesis, research paper, or article, Bookends provides the tools you need to manage your sources professionally and efficiently.
